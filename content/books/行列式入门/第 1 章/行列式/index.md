---
title: 行列式
---

本节，我要定义本章的主角，行列式（hánglièshì）。
注意，我并不为每个阵定义行列式；
我只为方阵定义行列式。

::: definition [行列式]
设 \(A\) 是 \(n\) 级阵（\(n \geqslant 1\)）。
定义 \(A\) 的**行列式**
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \det {(A)} =
  \begin{dcases}
    [A]_{1,1},                                               & n = 1; \\
    \sum_{i = 1}^{n} {(-1)^{i+1} [A]_{i,1} \det {(A(i|1))}}, & n \geqslant 2.
  \end{dcases}
\end{aligned}
\]
:::

\(\det\) 来自 <lang en>determinant</lang>（英语）。

我们看 4 个例。

::: example
设 \(A = [a]\) 是一个 \(1\) 级阵。
根据定义，\(\det {(A)} = [A]_{1,1} = a\)。
:::

::: example
设
\(
    A =
    \begin{bmatrix}
      a & c \\
      b & d \\
    \end{bmatrix}
  \)
是一个 \(2\) 级阵。
根据定义，
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \det {(A)}
  = {} &
  (-1)^{1+1} [A]_{1,1} \det {(A(1|1))}
  + (-1)^{2+1} [A]_{2,1} \det {(A(2|1))}
  \\
  = {} &
  [A]_{1,1} \det {[[A]_{2,2}]}
  - [A]_{2,1} \det {[[A]_{1,2}]}
  \\
  = {} &
  [A]_{1,1} [A]_{2,2} - [A]_{2,1} [A]_{1,2}
  \\
  = {} &
  ad - bc.
\end{aligned}
\]
此事是重要的；
我们会常用它。
通俗地，我们可用对角线记
\(2\) 级阵的行列式。

```tikz
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth]
    \matrix [
        matrix of math nodes,
        column sep=1em,
        row sep=1em
    ] (s2) {
        a & c \\
        b & d \\
    };
    \path
    (s2-1-1)    edge[->]        (s2-2-2)
    (s2-2-1)    edge[->,dashed] (s2-1-2);
    \node[anchor=east] at (s2-1-1.west) {\(+\)};
    \node[anchor=east] at (s2-2-1.west) {\(-\)};
\end{tikzpicture}
```

:::

::: example
设 \(A\) 是一个 \(3\) 级阵。
根据定义与上个例的结果，
\[
\begin{aligned}
  & \det {(A)}
  \\
  = {} &
  \hphantom{{} + {}}
  (-1)^{1+1} [A]_{1,1} \det {(A(1|1))}
  + (-1)^{2+1} [A]_{2,1} \det {(A(2|1))}
  \\
  &
  + (-1)^{3+1} [A]_{3,1} \det {(A(3|1))}
  \\
  = {} &
  \hphantom{{} + {}}
  [A]_{1,1}
  \det {
    \begin{bmatrix}
      [A]_{2,2} & [A]_{2,3} \\
      [A]_{3,2} & [A]_{3,3} \\
  \end{bmatrix}}
  - [A]_{2,1}
  \det {
    \begin{bmatrix}
      [A]_{1,2} & [A]_{1,3} \\
      [A]_{3,2} & [A]_{3,3} \\
  \end{bmatrix}}
  \\
  &
  + [A]_{3,1}
  \det {
    \begin{bmatrix}
      [A]_{1,2} & [A]_{1,3} \\
      [A]_{2,2} & [A]_{2,3} \\
  \end{bmatrix}}
  \\
  = {} &
  \hphantom{{} + {}}
  [A]_{1,1}
  ([A]_{2,2} [A]_{3,3} - [A]_{3,2} [A]_{2,3})
  - [A]_{2,1}
  ([A]_{1,2} [A]_{3,3} - [A]_{3,2} [A]_{1,3})
  \\
  &
  + [A]_{3,1}
  ([A]_{1,2} [A]_{2,3} - [A]_{2,2} [A]_{1,3})
  \\
  = {} &
  \hphantom{{} + {}}
  [A]_{1,1} [A]_{2,2} [A]_{3,3}
  - [A]_{1,1} [A]_{3,2} [A]_{2,3}
  \\
  &
  - [A]_{2,1} [A]_{1,2} [A]_{3,3}
  + [A]_{2,1} [A]_{3,2} [A]_{1,3}
  \\
  &
  + [A]_{3,1} [A]_{1,2} [A]_{2,3}
  - [A]_{3,1} [A]_{2,2} [A]_{1,3}
  \\
  = {} &
  \hphantom{{} + {}}
  [A]_{1,1} [A]_{2,2} [A]_{3,3}
  + [A]_{2,1} [A]_{3,2} [A]_{1,3}
  + [A]_{3,1} [A]_{1,2} [A]_{2,3}
  \\
  &
  - [A]_{1,1} [A]_{3,2} [A]_{2,3}
  - [A]_{2,1} [A]_{1,2} [A]_{3,3}
  - [A]_{3,1} [A]_{2,2} [A]_{1,3}.
\end{aligned}
\]
法国数学人 Pierre Frédéric Sarrus
给了我们一个记 \(3\) 级阵的行列式的好方法。
我们在阵的下面重写此阵。
由左上至右下的对角线（实线）上的数的积的和
减由左下至右上的对角线（虚线）上的数的积的和
即为此阵的行列式。

```tikz
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth]
    \matrix [
        matrix of math nodes,
        column sep=1em,
        row sep=1em
    ] (s3) {
        {[}A{]}_{1,1} & {[}A{]}_{1,2} & {[}A{]}_{1,3} \\
        {[}A{]}_{2,1} & {[}A{]}_{2,2} & {[}A{]}_{2,3} \\
        {[}A{]}_{3,1} & {[}A{]}_{3,2} & {[}A{]}_{3,3} \\
        {[}A{]}_{1,1} & {[}A{]}_{1,2} & {[}A{]}_{1,3} \\
        {[}A{]}_{2,1} & {[}A{]}_{2,2} & {[}A{]}_{2,3} \\
        {[}A{]}_{3,1} & {[}A{]}_{3,2} & {[}A{]}_{3,3} \\
    };
    \path
    (s3-1-1)    edge            (s3-2-2)
    (s3-2-2)    edge[->]        (s3-3-3)
    (s3-2-1)    edge            (s3-3-2)
    (s3-3-2)    edge[->]        (s3-4-3)
    (s3-3-1)    edge            (s3-4-2)
    (s3-4-2)    edge[->]        (s3-5-3)
    (s3-4-1)    edge[dashed]    (s3-3-2)
    (s3-3-2)    edge[->,dashed] (s3-2-3)
    (s3-5-1)    edge[dashed]    (s3-4-2)
    (s3-4-2)    edge[->,dashed] (s3-3-3)
    (s3-6-1)    edge[dashed]    (s3-5-2)
    (s3-5-2)    edge[->,dashed] (s3-4-3);
    \foreach \r in {1,2,3}
    {\node[anchor=east] at (s3-\r-1.west) {\(+\)};}
    \foreach \r in {4,5,6}
    {\node[anchor=east] at (s3-\r-1.west) {\(-\)};}
\end{tikzpicture}
```

:::

不过，注意，
前面的对角线法则无法被推广到
\(n\) 级阵（\(n \geqslant 4\)）的行列式。

::: example {@emp:DetOfSquareMatrixOfSize4}
设 \(A\) 是一个 \(4\) 级阵。
根据定义，
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \det {(A)}
  = {} &
  \hphantom{{} + {}}
  (-1)^{1+1} [A]_{1,1} \det {(A(1|1))}
  + (-1)^{2+1} [A]_{2,1} \det {(A(2|1))}
  \\
  &
  + (-1)^{3+1} [A]_{3,1} \det {(A(3|1))}
  + (-1)^{4+1} [A]_{4,1} \det {(A(4|1))}.
\end{aligned}
\]
由上个例，
\[
\begin{aligned}
  &
  \det {(A(1|1))}
  = \det {
    \begin{bmatrix}
      [A]_{2,2} & [A]_{2,3} & [A]_{2,4} \\
      [A]_{3,2} & [A]_{3,3} & [A]_{3,4} \\
      [A]_{4,2} & [A]_{4,3} & [A]_{4,4} \\
    \end{bmatrix}
  }
  \\
  = {} &
  \hphantom{{} + {}}
  [A]_{2,2} [A]_{3,3} [A]_{4,4}
  + [A]_{3,2} [A]_{4,3} [A]_{2,4}
  + [A]_{4,2} [A]_{2,3} [A]_{3,4}
  \\
  &
  - [A]_{2,2} [A]_{4,3} [A]_{3,4}
  - [A]_{3,2} [A]_{2,3} [A]_{4,4}
  - [A]_{4,2} [A]_{3,3} [A]_{2,4},
  \\
  &
  \det {(A(2|1))}
  = \det {
    \begin{bmatrix}
      [A]_{1,2} & [A]_{1,3} & [A]_{1,4} \\
      [A]_{3,2} & [A]_{3,3} & [A]_{3,4} \\
      [A]_{4,2} & [A]_{4,3} & [A]_{4,4} \\
    \end{bmatrix}
  }
  \\
  = {} &
  \hphantom{{} + {}}
  [A]_{1,2} [A]_{3,3} [A]_{4,4}
  + [A]_{3,2} [A]_{4,3} [A]_{1,4}
  + [A]_{4,2} [A]_{1,3} [A]_{3,4}
  \\
  &
  - [A]_{1,2} [A]_{4,3} [A]_{3,4}
  - [A]_{3,2} [A]_{1,3} [A]_{4,4}
  - [A]_{4,2} [A]_{3,3} [A]_{1,4},
  \\
  &
  \det {(A(3|1))}
  = \det {
    \begin{bmatrix}
      [A]_{1,2} & [A]_{1,3} & [A]_{1,4} \\
      [A]_{2,2} & [A]_{2,3} & [A]_{2,4} \\
      [A]_{4,2} & [A]_{4,3} & [A]_{4,4} \\
    \end{bmatrix}
  }
  \\
  = {} &
  \hphantom{{} + {}}
  [A]_{1,2} [A]_{2,3} [A]_{4,4}
  + [A]_{2,2} [A]_{4,3} [A]_{1,4}
  + [A]_{4,2} [A]_{1,3} [A]_{2,4}
  \\
  &
  - [A]_{1,2} [A]_{4,3} [A]_{2,4}
  - [A]_{2,2} [A]_{1,3} [A]_{4,4}
  - [A]_{4,2} [A]_{2,3} [A]_{1,4},
  \\
  &
  \det {(A(4|1))}
  = \det {
    \begin{bmatrix}
      [A]_{1,2} & [A]_{1,3} & [A]_{1,4} \\
      [A]_{2,2} & [A]_{2,3} & [A]_{2,4} \\
      [A]_{3,2} & [A]_{3,3} & [A]_{3,4} \\
    \end{bmatrix}
  }
  \\
  = {} &
  \hphantom{{} + {}}
  [A]_{1,2} [A]_{2,3} [A]_{3,4}
  + [A]_{2,2} [A]_{3,3} [A]_{1,4}
  + [A]_{3,2} [A]_{1,3} [A]_{2,4}
  \\
  &
  - [A]_{1,2} [A]_{3,3} [A]_{2,4}
  - [A]_{2,2} [A]_{1,3} [A]_{3,4}
  - [A]_{3,2} [A]_{2,3} [A]_{1,4}.
\end{aligned}
\]
则 \(\det {(A)}\) 等于
\[
\begin{aligned}
  & \hphantom{{} + {}}
  [A]_{1,1} [A]_{2,2} [A]_{3,3} [A]_{4,4}
  + [A]_{1,1} [A]_{3,2} [A]_{4,3} [A]_{2,4}
  + [A]_{1,1} [A]_{4,2} [A]_{2,3} [A]_{3,4}
  \\
  &
  - [A]_{1,1} [A]_{2,2} [A]_{4,3} [A]_{3,4}
  - [A]_{1,1} [A]_{3,2} [A]_{2,3} [A]_{4,4}
  - [A]_{1,1} [A]_{4,2} [A]_{3,3} [A]_{2,4}
  \\
  &
  - [A]_{2,1} [A]_{1,2} [A]_{3,3} [A]_{4,4}
  - [A]_{2,1} [A]_{3,2} [A]_{4,3} [A]_{1,4}
  - [A]_{2,1} [A]_{4,2} [A]_{1,3} [A]_{3,4}
  \\
  &
  + [A]_{2,1} [A]_{1,2} [A]_{4,3} [A]_{3,4}
  + [A]_{2,1} [A]_{3,2} [A]_{1,3} [A]_{4,4}
  + [A]_{2,1} [A]_{4,2} [A]_{3,3} [A]_{1,4}
  \\
  &
  + [A]_{3,1} [A]_{1,2} [A]_{2,3} [A]_{4,4}
  + [A]_{3,1} [A]_{2,2} [A]_{4,3} [A]_{1,4}
  + [A]_{3,1} [A]_{4,2} [A]_{1,3} [A]_{2,4}
  \\
  &
  - [A]_{3,1} [A]_{1,2} [A]_{4,3} [A]_{2,4}
  - [A]_{3,1} [A]_{2,2} [A]_{1,3} [A]_{4,4}
  - [A]_{3,1} [A]_{4,2} [A]_{2,3} [A]_{1,4}
  \\
  &
  - [A]_{4,1} [A]_{1,2} [A]_{2,3} [A]_{3,4}
  - [A]_{4,1} [A]_{2,2} [A]_{3,3} [A]_{1,4}
  - [A]_{4,1} [A]_{3,2} [A]_{1,3} [A]_{2,4}
  \\
  &
  + [A]_{4,1} [A]_{1,2} [A]_{3,3} [A]_{2,4}
  + [A]_{4,1} [A]_{2,2} [A]_{1,3} [A]_{3,4}
  \underline{{}
  + [A]_{4,1} [A]_{3,2} [A]_{2,3} [A]_{1,4}}.
\end{aligned}
\]
根据对角线法则，
我们似乎应减由左下至右上的对角线上的数的积。
可是，由上式，
\([A]_{4,1} [A]_{3,2} [A]_{2,3} [A]_{1,4}\)
前的符号是 \(+\)，
而不是 \(-\)。

```tikz
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth]
    \matrix [
        matrix of math nodes,
        column sep=1em,
        row sep=1em
    ] (s4) {
        {[}A{]}_{1,1} & {[}A{]}_{1,2} &
        {[}A{]}_{1,3} & {[}A{]}_{1,4} \\
        {[}A{]}_{2,1} & {[}A{]}_{2,2} &
        {[}A{]}_{2,3} & {[}A{]}_{2,4} \\
        {[}A{]}_{3,1} & {[}A{]}_{3,2} &
        {[}A{]}_{3,3} & {[}A{]}_{3,4} \\
        {[}A{]}_{4,1} & {[}A{]}_{4,2} &
        {[}A{]}_{4,3} & {[}A{]}_{4,4} \\
    };
    \path
    (s4-4-1)    edge[]    (s4-3-2)
    (s4-3-2)    edge[]    (s4-2-3)
    (s4-2-3)    edge[->]  (s4-1-4);
    \node[anchor=north] at (s4-4-1.south)
    {\(+\)};
\end{tikzpicture}
```

我不要求您记如此具体的公式，
因为 \(n\) 级阵（\(n \geqslant 4\)）的行列式的具体的公式
不如 \(1\) 级阵、\(2\) 级阵、\(3\) 级阵
的行列式的具体的公式实用。
:::
